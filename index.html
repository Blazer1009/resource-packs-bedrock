How do I use these resource packs?

Download the .mcpack or .mcaddon file and open it to automatically import into Minecraft Bedrock. You can also place extracted packs into the "resource_packs" and/or the "behavior_packs" folder inside the com.mojang directory.

Do I need addons or mods?

No. These are vanilla Minecraft Bedrock resource packs unless stated otherwise.

How does the ticket pack work?

Rename Light 0 in an anvil to "Ticket" and it will turn into a custom ticket item. Perfect for servers, events, and maps.

Why do some packs have two versions?

The standard version works on all Bedrock devices, while the enhanced version includes extra visuals or experimental features (when supported).

Can I use these on servers?

Yes. They work on Bedrock servers and worlds that allow resource packs.
